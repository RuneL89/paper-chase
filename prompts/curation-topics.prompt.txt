=== TASK ===
Below is the COMPLETE topic set of the wiki: every topic aggregated from the current extraction set plus every topic that already exists on disk (update runs re-curate everything). Each candidate carries its slug, title, folder, claim count, and up to 3 sample claims as evidence.

Decide the fate of EVERY candidate topic, exactly once:

- MERGE: two or more candidates are the SAME theme under different wording, plural/singular form, or spelling (e.g. "external-factor" and "external-factors"). The merge target ("into") absorbs the "from" slugs; its title and folder win.
- DROP: the candidate is NOT a theme, concept, or issue a journalist would search for. Meta-descriptors of the documents' rhetoric — e.g. "statistical", "temporal", "methodological", "definitional", "trend" — are not topics. Dropping a topic never deletes evidence: its claims remain on the entity and document pages.
- KEEP: everything else. Kept candidates need no decision from you — every candidate you do not list is kept automatically.

Rules:
- Every candidate slug you list must appear in EXACTLY ONE place: inside one merge entry's "from" list, as one merge entry's "into", or in "drop". Every candidate NOT listed is kept automatically — there is no "keep" list to write.
- A merge entry's "into" slug must NOT also appear in "drop" — being the merge target already means it is kept.
- Never merge into a slug that you are also dropping. Chained merges (A into B, B into C) are allowed — they resolve to the final survivor.
- Never merge a slug into itself.
- Judge from the sample claims, not from the slug alone.
- When in doubt, KEEP (simply leave the candidate unlisted). A false merge or false drop is worse than a redundant topic.
- Do not justify your decisions. If a justification is truly unavoidable, cap it at a few words.

Proposed pairs:
- The PROPOSED PAIRS section (when present) lists candidate pairs already flagged by deterministic pre-merge signals, each with its signal and evidence. Judge every proposed pair: CONFIRM the merge when the pair truly is the same theme in the proposed direction; DENY otherwise — a denial keeps both candidates, exactly like leaving them unlisted.
- Proposed-pair slugs are NOT in the CANDIDATES list and must NOT appear in "merge" or "drop" — judge them ONLY in the "pairs" output array.
- A pair you do not judge is treated as denied. Give a few words of justification per judgment, capped at a few words.
- When in doubt about a proposed pair, DENY. A false merge is worse than a redundant topic.

Return ONLY a single JSON object — no markdown fences, no commentary, no explanation:

Empty arrays are valid when nothing merges or nothing drops.
