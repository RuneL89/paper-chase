## Purpose

- This wiki is a citation-backed markdown knowledge base generated from the PDFs in `raw/`.
- Wiki title: **{{WIKI_TITLE}}**
- Wiki slug: `{{SLUG}}`
- Every fact in this wiki must be traceable back to a source PDF and page.

## Page Structure

- Pages live under `pages/` in topic/entity folders (e.g. `pages/entities/people/`, `pages/topics/`).
- Every page is markdown with YAML frontmatter:
  ```markdown
  ---
  title: Page Title
  type: entity | topic | synthesis
  sources:
    - id: src1
      pdf: source-file-name
      pages: [1, 2]
  ---
  ```
- Pages link to each other with `[[Page Title]]` wikilinks.
- Raw extracted text lives in `raw-pages/` and is never edited by hand.

## Citation Rules

- Every claim derived from a source must carry a footnote citation in the form `[^srcN]`, where `N` matches a source listed in the page's `sources` frontmatter.
- Each `[^srcN]` footnote resolves to a source entry with the PDF name and page number(s).
- A claim with no citation must be marked as uncited or removed during validation.
- Never invent sources, page numbers, or quotes.
